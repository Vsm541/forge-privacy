Forge Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

Forge is a private personal fitness and health tracking application designed to help users track workouts, fitness information, weight, blood pressure, and related health and wellness information.

Information Forge Uses

Forge may process information entered directly by the user, including workout information, weight, blood pressure, and other fitness or health information.

With the user's permission, Forge may also access selected health and fitness information through Apple HealthKit and connected fitness services such as WHOOP.

Apple Health and HealthKit

Forge only accesses Apple Health information after the user grants permission through Apple's HealthKit authorization system.

Health information obtained through HealthKit is used only to provide fitness and health tracking functionality within Forge. Forge does not use HealthKit information for advertising, marketing, or data brokerage purposes.

WHOOP

If WHOOP integration is enabled, Forge may access authorized fitness and recovery information from the user's WHOOP account. Access occurs only after authorization by the user.

Data Storage

Forge is designed using a local-first architecture. Information entered directly into Forge is stored locally on the user's device where applicable. Some functionality may communicate with authorized third-party services when necessary to provide integrations requested by the user.

Data Sharing

Forge does not sell personal information and does not use personal health or fitness information for third-party advertising or targeted advertising.

User Control

Users control what information they enter into Forge and which external services they authorize Forge to access. Permissions for services such as Apple Health can be changed or revoked through the applicable device or service settings.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Forge. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Forge's functionality changes. Updates will be published at this location with a revised effective date.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the developer through the contact information associated with Forge's distribution.
